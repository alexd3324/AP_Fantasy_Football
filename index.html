Expected wins
Each week a team's score is compared with every other team's score, as if it played all of them. Beating 8 of 11 teams counts as 0.73 of a win. The weekly values add up over the season.
Wins above expected
Actual wins minus expected wins. Positive means a team has won more than its scoring deserves. Negative means it has been unlucky.
Schedule luck
The league's average score minus the average score of the opponents a team actually faced, in points per game. Positive means an easier schedule than average.
